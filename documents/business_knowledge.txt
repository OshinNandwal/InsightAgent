INSIGHTAGENT BUSINESS KNOWLEDGE

Revenue:
Revenue is the total amount of money generated from product sales before deducting costs.

Profit:
Profit represents the amount remaining after relevant costs are deducted from revenue.

Profit Margin:
Profit margin is calculated as:
Profit Margin = (Profit / Revenue) × 100

Units Sold:
Units sold represents the total number of products sold during a given period.

Regional Performance:
Regional performance can be compared using revenue, profit, units sold, and profit margin.

Product Performance:
Product performance should consider both sales volume and profitability.

Revenue and Profit:
Revenue should be analyzed together with profit because high revenue does not always mean high profitability.

Declining Revenue:
A decline in revenue should be investigated by comparing regions, products, categories, and time periods.

Business Recommendations:
Recommendations should be based on measurable evidence from the available data.

Facts and Explanations:
When answering analytical questions, clearly distinguish between observed facts and possible explanations.

Data Limitations:
If the available data is insufficient to support a conclusion, the system should explicitly state that limitation.

Business Question: Highest Revenue Region:
To identify the highest revenue region, compare total revenue across all available regions.

Business Question: Highest Profit Product:
To identify the highest profit product, compare total profit across all available products.

Business Question: Strongest Sales Month:
To identify the strongest sales month, compare total revenue across the available months.

Business Question: Highest Profit Margin:
To identify products with the highest profit margins, compare profit margin calculated from total profit divided by total revenue.

Business Question: Management Focus:
Management focus should be determined using measurable indicators such as revenue, profit, units sold, and profit margin.

Business Question: Revenue Change:
Revenue changes should be investigated by comparing time periods, regions, products, and categories.
